Portal users with access to a project see its timesheets: the timesheet
list of the portal, the timesheets and time spent of each task, the task
timesheet report and, with sales, the timesheets of their orders and
invoices. This module adds a per-project option to hide them. When it is
unchecked, the portal and the project sharing views show neither the
timesheet lines of the project nor the allocated time, time spent, time
remaining and progress of its tasks. The option is checked by default,
so the portal keeps showing timesheets until it is unchecked.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *Project > Configuration > Projects* and open a project with
   **Timesheets** enabled and its **Visibility** set to invited portal
   users.
2. In the **Settings** tab, uncheck **Timesheets in Portal** to hide its
   timesheets from the portal, or keep it checked to show them.
