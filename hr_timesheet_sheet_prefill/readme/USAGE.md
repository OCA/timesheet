1. Open a timesheet sheet in **new** or **draft** state.
2. Click the **Prefill from Previous Period** button.
3. The module looks up the employee's most recent confirmed or approved timesheet sheet
   and copies its project/task combinations as empty (zero-hour) lines into the current
   sheet.

Project/task combinations that already exist in the current sheet are skipped, so
pressing the button multiple times will not create duplicates.
