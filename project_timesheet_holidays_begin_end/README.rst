When a time off request is validated, or a public holiday is created,
Odoo books a timesheet entry on the internal project for every working
day off, carrying just the hours of that day. With
``hr_timesheet_time_control`` such an entry also gets a start and end
time, and as nothing sets them they end up being the time of day the
entry was generated.

This module starts these generated entries with the working hours of the
employee: the entry begins with the first attendance of the day that
falls in the time off and lasts the hours of the day. A morning or full
day off starts with the morning, an afternoon off with the afternoon. As
breaks are not counted, the entry ends before the working hours do.

Usage
=====

Set up time off timesheets as usual: in *Timesheets > Configuration >
Settings*, enable *Time Off* and choose the internal project and task
the leaves are booked on.

From then on, once a time off request is validated, or a public holiday
is created, the generated timesheet entries start with the working
schedule of the employee. With a schedule of 08:00-12:00 and
13:00-17:00:

-  A full day of leave gives one entry from 08:00 lasting 8 hours.
-  A morning off gives one entry from 08:00 lasting 4 hours, an
   afternoon off one entry from 13:00 lasting 4 hours.
-  A leave taken in hours, e.g. from 09:00 to 15:00, gives one entry
   from 09:00 lasting the 5 working hours in that period.

Public holidays and other days off inside a leave stay without entries,
as before. Employees on a flexible working schedule have no attendances
to follow, so their entries keep the standard behaviour. The entries are
still deleted along with the request when it is refused or cancelled.
