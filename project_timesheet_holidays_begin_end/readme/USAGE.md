Set up time off timesheets as usual: in *Timesheets > Configuration >
Settings*, enable *Time Off* and choose the internal project and task the
leaves are booked on.

From then on, once a time off request is validated, or a public holiday is
created, the generated timesheet entries start with the working schedule of
the employee. With a schedule of 08:00-12:00 and 13:00-17:00:

- A full day of leave gives one entry from 08:00 lasting 8 hours.
- A morning off gives one entry from 08:00 lasting 4 hours, an afternoon off
  one entry from 13:00 lasting 4 hours.
- A leave taken in hours, e.g. from 09:00 to 15:00, gives one entry from
  09:00 lasting the 5 working hours in that period.

Public holidays and other days off inside a leave stay without entries, as
before. Employees on a flexible working schedule have no attendances to
follow, so their entries keep the standard behaviour. The entries are still
deleted along with the request when it is refused or cancelled.
