The hours on **Timesheets Report** are computed from **Quantity rounded** on the timesheet lines, converted to hours.

**Report on selected lines**

1. Open the **Timesheets** app.
2. Open **My Timesheets**, or open **Timesheets** and then **All Timesheets**.
3. Select the timesheet lines to include.
4. Click **Action**, then **Generate Timesheet Report**.
5. Click **View**, **Export to PDF**, or **Export to XLSX**.

**Report from the wizard**

**Timesheet Report Wizard** is visible to users in the group **User: all timesheets**. If the menu is missing, that group is not assigned.

1. Open the **Timesheets** app.
2. Open **Reporting**.
3. Open **Timesheet Report Wizard**.
4. Set the dates, projects, tasks, employees, tags, and departments to include.
5. Click **View**, **Export to PDF**, or **Export to XLSX**.
